## 🧭 How I Use These Resources

These resources are intended to **support learning**, not replace hands-on practice.

### 📖 Learn

Understand the mathematical or technical concept.

### 💻 Implement

Write the concept from scratch whenever possible.

### 🧪 Experiment

Test different approaches and observe their behavior.

### 📝 Document

Create personal notes, examples, and implementations.

### 🚀 Build

Apply the knowledge to projects and real-world problems.

## 📌 Resource Philosophy

> **Quality > Quantity**

I prefer maintaining a small collection of **high-value, trustworthy resources** rather than collecting hundreds of random links.

A resource should ideally help with at least one of the following:

* 📖 Learning a concept
* 🔄 Revising a concept
* 💻 Implementing a concept
* 🧠 Developing deeper understanding
* 🔬 Exploring advanced topics
* 🛠️ Building real projects
