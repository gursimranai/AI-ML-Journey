# Free Courses

This directory contains free courses and learning resources for programming, mathematics, data science, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, and software engineering.

The goal is to build strong fundamentals through structured learning, practical exercises, and projects without relying entirely on paid courses.

**Note:** Course access, free tiers, certificates, and platform policies can change. Check the official course page for current details.

## 11. Recommended Learning Order

A practical progression for a beginner interested in AI/ML is:

1. **Python:** Learn the language and solve programming exercises.
2. **Mathematics:** Study linear algebra, probability, statistics, and calculus fundamentals.
3. **Data analysis:** Practice NumPy, Pandas, visualization, and SQL.
4. **Machine learning:** Learn classical algorithms, preprocessing, and evaluation.
5. **Deep learning:** Study neural networks and PyTorch.
6. **Specialization:** Choose NLP, computer vision, or another relevant area.
7. **Generative AI:** Learn transformers, embeddings, RAG, and model evaluation.
8. **Engineering:** Add testing, APIs, Git, Docker, and deployment.
9. **Projects:** Build end-to-end systems and document the results.

You can begin small projects during the early stages. Do not wait until you finish every course before applying what you learn.

## 12. How to Study Effectively

For each course:

- Read or watch one lesson at a time.
- Reproduce the examples yourself.
- Complete the exercises without immediately looking at solutions.
- Write concise notes for difficult concepts.
- Build a small project related to the topic.
- Record useful references in your repository.
- Revisit topics that you cannot explain or implement independently.

Avoid collecting courses without completing them. One well-understood course combined with meaningful practice is usually more valuable than several unfinished courses.
