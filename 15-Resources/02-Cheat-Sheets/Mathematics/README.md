## Cheat Sheet Guidelines

Each reference should include:

- **Formula:** Write the expression clearly.
- **Variable definitions:** Explain every symbol.
- **Conditions:** State when the formula applies.
- **Method:** Provide the steps needed to use it.
- **Example:** Include at least one worked problem.
- **Common mistakes:** Highlight sign errors, incorrect substitutions, and other frequent pitfalls.

## Usage

Use these references for exam preparation, assignment solving, interview revision, and understanding the mathematics behind AI/ML algorithms.

Prioritize understanding the derivation and conditions of a formula instead of memorizing it alone. Verify results through substitution, estimation, or an independent calculation when possible.

This directory supports the mathematical foundations required for Computer Science, Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.
