# 🎧 Audio And Speech Data

A collection of audio and speech datasets for audio processing, speech recognition, sound classification, and machine learning experiments.

This directory contains datasets consisting of recorded sounds, spoken language, music, environmental audio, and other audio signals.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand digital audio data and its structure.
- Learn audio loading, playback, and visualization.
- Practice audio cleaning and preprocessing.
- Explore speech and sound classification.
- Prepare audio datasets for machine learning and deep learning.

---

## 📁 Dataset Categories

Organize datasets into the following categories when needed:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| `01-Speech-Recognition/` | Spoken audio with corresponding text transcripts. |
| `02-Speaker-Identification/` | Audio recordings used to identify or distinguish speakers. |
| `03-Emotion-Recognition/` | Speech recordings labeled with emotional categories. |
| `04-Music-Datasets/` | Music recordings, genres, instruments, and related audio data. |
| `05-Environmental-Sounds/` | Sounds from nature, cities, machines, and everyday environments. |
| `06-Voice-Commands/` | Short speech recordings containing commands or keywords. |
| `07-Audio-Classification/` | Labeled audio samples for classification experiments. |
| `08-Other-Audio-Datasets/` | Other audio datasets that do not fit the above categories. |

- Audio file formats
- Sample rate and number of channels, where known
- Number and duration of recordings
- Language and transcription details, where applicable
- Label or annotation information
- Preprocessing history
- Potential applications

---

## 📌 Data Management Guidelines

1. Preserve original audio recordings separately from processed versions.
2. Document sample rates, channels, and audio formats.
3. Keep transcripts and labels aligned with their corresponding recordings.
4. Record any resampling, trimming, or noise-reduction operations.
5. Respect speaker consent, privacy, and dataset usage restrictions.
6. Verify licensing and redistribution permissions before uploading audio files.
