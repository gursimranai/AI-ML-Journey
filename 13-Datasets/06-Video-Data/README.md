# 🎬 Video Data

A collection of video-based datasets for video processing, action recognition, motion analysis, and computer vision experiments.

This directory contains datasets consisting of video recordings, clips, frames, and associated annotations or metadata.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand video data structures and formats.
- Learn video loading, playback, and frame extraction.
- Practice video preprocessing and transformation.
- Explore motion detection and action recognition.
- Prepare video datasets for machine learning and deep learning.

---

## 📁 Dataset Categories

Organize datasets into the following categories when needed:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| `01-Action-Recognition/` | Videos labeled with human actions and activities. |
| `02-Human-Activity-Recognition/` | Videos depicting everyday human activities and movements. |
| `03-Object-Tracking/` | Videos with object identities and tracking annotations. |
| `04-Traffic-And-Vehicles/` | Road traffic, vehicle movement, and transportation videos. |
| `05-Surveillance-And-Security/` | Video datasets for surveillance-related research, subject to privacy restrictions. |
| `06-Sports-Videos/` | Sports recordings for activity, event, and movement analysis. |
| `07-Video-Summarization/` | Videos with summaries or annotations for summarization tasks. |
| `08-Other-Video-Datasets/` | Other video datasets that do not fit the above categories. |

- Video formats and resolutions
- Frame rates and duration, where known
- Number of videos and classes
- Annotation and labeling details
- Preprocessing history
- Potential applications

---

## 📌 Data Management Guidelines

1. Preserve original video files separately from processed versions.
2. Document frame rates, resolutions, codecs, and durations whenever available.
3. Keep annotations aligned with the correct videos and frames.
4. Document frame extraction and video transformation procedures.
5. Consider storage requirements before committing large video files to GitHub.
6. Respect privacy, consent, copyright, and licensing requirements.
7. Never redistribute restricted video content without permission.
